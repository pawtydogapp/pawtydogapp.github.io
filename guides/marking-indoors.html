Marking indoors

All behaviour guides

Why it happens

Small, deliberate urine deposits on vertical spots, communication, not emptying. More common in intact males and in multi-dog homes.

What works

  1. Rule out medical causes first, a urine test is cheap certainty.
  2. Supervise, interrupt calmly mid-lift and head straight out, pay outdoor marking.
  3. Enzymatic cleaning of every historical spot, old scent is an invitation.

What makes it worse

  • Do not punish found marks, it increases sneaky marking.
  • Belly bands manage laundry, they do not train anything on their own.

Keep this in mind

Marking responds well to the supervise-clean-redirect triangle. Neutering reduces it in many but not all males, discuss timing with your vet.

What owners usually need for this

  • Enzymatic cleaner
  • Belly band

When to get help in person

Pawty is educational content, not veterinary or behavioural advice. See a veterinarian for any sudden behaviour change or sign of pain, and a qualified behaviourist in person if your dog has bitten, guards with snapping, or panics when left alone. Read our approach.
