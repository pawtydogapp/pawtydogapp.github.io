The story

Pawty is a free "Duolingo for dogs" built by Matej, a Slovenian banking professional, alongside Nala, his cockapoo puppy. Frustrated by paywalled and guilt-driven training apps, he built the opposite: 90+ skills taught in 5-minute daily sessions rated with paw prints, streaks that freeze instead of reset, behaviour guides whose core message is "this is normal", a care calendar, a food diary, and a worldwide activity leaderboard where every breed can win.

Fast facts

Free with a small banner ad, no subscription. 90+ skills, 28 behaviour guides, 12-week starter plan. Data hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). Android first, iOS following. Built in Slovenia, tested by Nala.
