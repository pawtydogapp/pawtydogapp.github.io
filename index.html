Why Pawty is different

💬

It says the behaviour is normal

31 of the 32 behaviour guides open by saying so, because usually it is true. Most training content is written to make you feel behind.

🧊

Falling behind is designed in

Streaks freeze instead of resetting. The 12-week plan has review weeks built into it. No red badges, and nothing telling you that you have let your dog down.

🐕

Tested on a real dog first

Nala is a cockapoo, food motivated, and completely deaf to recall when there is a squirrel. Every exercise is tried on her before it ships.
