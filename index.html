Why Pawty is different

The guides are written to calm you down

Your dog is not going to behave like the ones in films, and that is fine. The behaviour guides exist to take the stress, guilt and worry out of it, then tell you what to actually do.

Falling behind is designed in

Streaks freeze instead of resetting. No red badges, and nothing telling you that you have let your dog down.

Tested on a real dog first

Nala is a cockapoo, food motivated, and completely deaf to recall when there is a squirrel. Every exercise is tried on her before it ships.

Everything is unlocked

No premium tier, no locked lessons, no trial that ends. Every skill, guide and plan is open from the first minute. Most training apps put the useful half behind 8 to 15 EUR a month.
