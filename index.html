Our promise

Your dog is not broken and you are not failing.

Your dog is not going to behave like the ones in films, and that is fine. The behaviour guides exist to take the stress, guilt and worry out of it, then tell you what to actually do.

Questions

Is there a paid version?

No. Every skill, guide and feature is available to everyone. A single banner advert covers the server costs, and the Marketplace earns a commission when someone buys through it.

Does it work for puppies and adult dogs?

Yes. There is a puppy corner for the first weeks at home, a 12-week puppy plan and an 8-week adult plan for dogs of any age.

Is the training advice safe for my dog?

Pawty uses positive reinforcement only, with no aversive tools. It is educational content, not veterinary or behavioural advice. Read our approach for the full picture.

Which languages are available?

Pawty is available in 11 languages: English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Hrvatski, Italiano, Nederlands, Polski, Português, Română, Slovenščina.
