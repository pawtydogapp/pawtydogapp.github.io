Why fleece

Gentle in the mouth

Soft fleece is gentle on teeth and gums during supervised tug play, with a cushioned grip for dogs that pull hard.

Quiet indoors

No heavy knots and no plastic parts, so play stays quiet and kind to your floors.

Easy to wash

Machine washable on a sensitive programme at 30 degrees.

Safety

  • For supervised tug play only. This is not a chew toy.
  • Put the tug away after play, out of your dog's reach.
  • Check the tug before each use. Replace it if it is worn or torn, or if pieces come loose.
  • If your dog swallows fleece, contact your vet.
  • This is a dog toy, not a toy for children.
