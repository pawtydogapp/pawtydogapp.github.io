Common questions

Is there a paid version?

No. Every skill, guide and feature is available to everyone. A single banner advert covers the server costs, and the Marketplace earns a commission when someone buys through it.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to More, then Account and profile, then Delete account. This removes your profile, dog, training history and photo permanently. If you cannot reach that screen, email us and we will delete it within 30 days.

I lost my streak. Can you restore it?

Streaks use a freeze rather than a reset, so a single missed day does not wipe it. If it reset anyway, email us the date and we will look at your account.

The clicker or whistle makes no sound.

On iPhone, check the silent switch on the side of the phone and the volume. If it still fails, close the app fully and reopen it, then press the button once before starting a session.

I cannot sign in with Google or Apple.

Try the email and password option instead, using the same email address. If you see a blank white screen after signing in, close the app fully and reopen it, then email us with the phone model.

My training progress disappeared.

Progress is stored on your account, not on the phone. Check you signed in with the same method you used originally. Signing in with Google when you first registered with email creates a second, empty account.

How do I change my dog's name, breed or photo?

More, then Account and profile. Changes appear on the leaderboard immediately.

What shows on the leaderboard?

Your country flag, dog's name, dog photo and XP. Nothing else. Your care calendar and food diary are private.

Can I use Pawty for more than 1 dog?

Yes. Add up to 6 dogs under More, then Account and profile, then Your dogs. Each dog keeps its own progress, badges and care calendar, and you switch between them with 1 tap.

Is the training advice safe for my dog?

Pawty uses positive reinforcement only, with no aversive tools. It is educational content, not veterinary or behavioural advice. Read our approach for the full picture.

What are the links in the Marketplace?

Some are affiliate links. If you buy through one, Pawty may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only list merchants we would use ourselves.

How do I report a mistake in a skill or guide?

Email us the skill name and what is wrong. We take this seriously, especially on the behaviour guides.
