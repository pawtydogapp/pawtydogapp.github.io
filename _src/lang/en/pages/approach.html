Our approach

How Pawty teaches

Positive reinforcement only

Every skill in Pawty is taught by rewarding the behaviour you want. Food, praise, play, or a marker sound from the built-in clicker or whistle.

Pawty does not use, teach or recommend aversive methods. No prong collars, no electric collars, no choke chains, no alpha rolls, no shouting, no physical corrections. Not as a last resort, not for stubborn dogs, not at all.

5 minutes at a time

Sessions in Pawty are 5 minutes long. Dogs learn better in short bursts than in long sessions.

You rate each session from 1 to 5 paws. Three good sessions in a row marks a skill as mastered. That is a deliberately low bar, because a dog that can sit reliably in your kitchen has genuinely learned something.

Falling behind is allowed

The 12-week starter plan has review weeks built into it. Streaks freeze instead of resetting. There are no red badges, no guilt messages, and no notification telling you that you have let your dog down.

Most dog training content is written as though the owner has unlimited time. Most owners do not. Pawty is written for the second group.

What Pawty is not

Pawty is educational content. It is not veterinary advice and it is not a substitute for a qualified trainer or behaviourist.

See a vet

See a veterinarian if your dog shows a sudden change in behaviour, signs of pain, or a health problem. Behaviour changes can have medical causes.

See a behaviourist in person

See a qualified behaviourist in person if your dog has bitten a person or another dog, guards food or objects with growling or snapping, shows aggression on the lead, or panics when left alone. These cases need someone in the room. They cannot be solved through an app, and we will not pretend otherwise.

You know your dog

You know your dog. If an exercise in Pawty makes your dog frightened or uncomfortable, stop it.

Found something wrong

If you are a trainer or behaviourist and you disagree with something in the app, write to info@pawtyapp.com. Tell us the skill and what is wrong with it. We will fix it and credit you if you want the credit.
